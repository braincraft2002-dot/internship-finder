Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

1. The short version. This site has no user accounts and no database of students. Almost everything you do here stays inside your own browser.

2. What stays in your browser. Your profile, bookmarks and tracker stay in your browser. We do not store them on a server. If you buy Premium or request a refund, we receive your email and UPI transaction ID, used only to verify payment. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them completely.

3. The contact form. If you send a message through the contact form on the homepage, the form is processed by Netlify Forms, and we receive the email address and message you typed. We use them only to reply to you. We do not sell, rent or share them, and we do not add you to any mailing list. You can ask for your message to be deleted at any time through the same form.

4. Payments. If Premium is active, payment happens directly between your UPI app and the owner. This site never sees, processes or stores your card number, bank details or UPI PIN.

5. Cookies and tracking. This site does not run advertising trackers or third-party analytics. The only storage it uses is the local storage described in section 2, which holds your own preferences.

6. Links to other sites. Every listing links out to the original publisher's website. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which we do not control. Check them before you apply or share personal details there.

7. Children. The site is a general study resource and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone; there is nothing to submit except the optional contact form.

8. Changes and contact. If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. For any privacy question, use the contact form on the homepage — or the owner email listed in the Terms of Use.
