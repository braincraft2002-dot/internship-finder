About and Policies

About: Internship Finder was built by Praveen, a student, as an independent student-built project — it is not affiliated with any university, company or government body. Every day it collects internships, research programmes, scholarships and fellowships from official sources and public feeds, matches them to your degree and field of study, and links you straight to the official posting.

What this is: an aggregator. We index opportunities from public feeds and official sites, linking directly to original postings. We never host applications or charge for internships; Premium pays for utility features.

Refund policy: refunded if no opportunity matching your degree appears in 30 days, or if activated by mistake. Contact the owner with your UPI transaction ID.

Privacy: Your profile, bookmarks and tracker stay in your browser. We do not store them on a server. If you buy Premium or request a refund, we receive your email and UPI transaction ID, used only to verify payment.
